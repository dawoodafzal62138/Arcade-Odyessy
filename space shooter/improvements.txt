1. Game States (Start & Game Over Screens)
Right now, running = False just kills the program. Instead, you should have a game_active boolean.

Menu State: A screen with the title and "Press Space to Start."

Game Over State: When you hit a meteor, show the final score and a "Press R to Restart" option. This keeps the player in the "loop" without having to re-run the Python script.

2. Parallax Background
Give the space a sense of depth by making the stars move.

Create two layers of stars.

Make the large, bright stars move faster than the small, dim stars. This creates a 3D effect called Parallax.

3. Meteor Variety
Instead of one meteor type, load a list of different meteor images.

Small Meteors: Move faster but are worth more points.

Big Meteors: Move slower but take 2 or 3 laser hits to destroy.

Scale: Use pygame.transform.scale_by() to randomize the size of the meteors as they spawn.

4. Power-ups
Occasionally, when a meteor is destroyed, have it drop a power-up sprite that moves down the screen.

Shield: Protects the player from one collision.

Rapid Fire: Decreases the cooldown_time for a few seconds.

Multi-shot: Fires three lasers at once in different directions.

5. Screen Shake
This is the ultimate way to add "Juice." When a meteor is destroyed or the player is hit, slightly offset the entire Display_surface by a few random pixels for a fraction of a second. It makes the explosions feel powerful.
